1. Overview

Method 365 turns your files into an agreement, sends it to each signer, and keeps the completed, digitally signed PDF with a full audit trail. Every agreement follows the same path:

RoleWhat they do
SenderPrepares and sends agreements; sees the ones they sent and those sent to them.
AdministratorAlso manages users, companies, support tickets, the system log and backups.
System administratorOne named administrator who can also update and restart the server.
Signer with an accountJRS staff: signs after signing in; can view agreements sent to them.
Outside signerSomeone without an account: signs with a temporary password emailed to them.

2. Getting started

Signing in

  1. Open https://esign.method365sign.com.
  2. Enter your email (your sign-in ID) and password. Click Show to check what you typed.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code emailed to you. It works once and expires after 10 minutes.
  • Your administrator creates your account and emails you your sign-in ID and password.
  • After 5 wrong passwords in a row your account locks for 15 minutes.
  • You're signed out after 30 minutes without activity.
Forgot your password or locked out? Click Ask an administrator for help on the sign-in page. You get a case number, and an administrator fixes it and emails you.

My profile

Click your name at the top right. Set your title, company and a default signature. The signing page offers them for you to confirm; they're never applied without your click.

3. Sending an agreement

Step 1: upload the files

On Documents, drop files on the upload box or click it. Choose several files of different types to merge them into one agreement.

You uploadWhat happens
PDFUsed as it is
Word, Excel, PowerPoint, textTurned into PDF pages. Check them: a missing font can shift the layout slightly.
Images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, TIFF, BMP)One A4 page per image, turned upright
iPhone HEIC photoRefused: share it as JPG instead

Step 2: add recipients and fields

  1. Under Add recipients, click + Add recipient and enter each person's full name and email.
  2. Choose the signing order: One after another (for “performed by → checked by → QA approval”) or Everyone at once.
  3. Under Fields for, pick the recipient, click a field type such as E-signature, then click on the page to place it. Drag to move; drag the corner to resize.
  4. Copy a field with Ctrl+C and paste with Ctrl+V.
Every recipient needs at least one E-signature field. Every signer must give a reason (the meaning of their signature); this can't be switched off.

Step 3: organize pages (optional)

Click Organize pages to drag pages into order, rotate or delete them, or + Add files to merge in more.

Step 4: send

Click Send. The agreement gets its ID (for example JRS-2026-00014) and the first signer(s) are emailed a personal link.

4. Tracking agreements

Open an agreement from Documents to see each recipient's status, their signing link and the audit trail.

StatusMeaning
DraftNot sent; you can still edit or discard it
Out for signatureSent; waiting for one or more signers
CompletedEveryone signed; the signed PDF is ready
DeclinedA signer declined, with a reason
CancelledCancelled by the sender or an administrator, with a reason
  • Send reminder emails the signer their link again.
  • Email from Outlook opens a ready-written email with the signer's link, from your own mailbox.
  • Copy (beside a link) lets you share it yourself. Each link works only for its invited person.
  • Cancel agreement asks for a reason. Sent agreements can't be deleted.

5. Signing an agreement

Only the person the agreement was sent to can sign it. How you prove who you are depends on whether you have a Method 365 account:

If you have an account

Open the link in the email. If you're not signed in, click Sign in as …; after sign-in you return to the document. If you're signed in as someone else, log out first.

If you don't have an account

Open the link. A temporary password is emailed to you. It works once, for this agreement only, for 60 minutes. Didn't get it? Click Send a new temporary password.

Signing step by step

  1. Read the document and tick the box agreeing to use electronic records and signatures.
  2. Click the first signature place and adopt your signature: use your saved one, draw, type or upload.
  3. Click each other signature place. Each signs on your click, with no retyping, so you see every place you sign. Next field takes you to the next one.
  4. Click Finish, confirm your title and company, and choose the reason for signing.
  5. Enter your password (account password or temporary password) and click Sign.
Declining: click Decline, give a reason and enter your password. The agreement closes and the sender is told.

Without an account, after signing you can click Yes, request an account; you get a case number and an administrator follows up.

6. Completed agreements

A completed agreement is a locked PDF, certified by Jupiter Research Services and digitally signed by each signer. Nobody can change or delete it.

  • Download signed PDF: opens without a password; it can be printed but its text can't be copied or edited.
  • Print copy: a stamped copy showing who printed it, when, and a link to verify it.
  • Audit trail: every event with date, time, who, IP address and how the person proved who they were. Choose From and To to see or download one period.

Verify a signed PDF

Anyone can check a signed PDF on the Verify page: an unchanged original shows as valid with its agreement ID and signers.

7. Administration

The Admin ▾ menu appears for administrators only. Everything done here is recorded in the system log.

Users

  • Add a user: name, email and access. Generate fills in a strong password; the person is emailed their sign-in details.
  • Edit, Set new password, and audit-trail View/Download permissions per user.
  • Deactivate instead of deleting, so names stay in every audit trail.
  • Outside signers lists people who signed without an account; Create account fills in the add-user form.

Support tickets

Sign-in problems and account requests arrive with a case number. Fix the problem, then Resolve with a note; the person is emailed.

System log

Sign-ins, failed attempts, locks, user changes, backups, restarts and outside signers' activity, with an integrity check. Filter by time and download as PDF.

Backup and restore

  • Download backup to this computer or Save a backup on the server now.
  • Automatic backups: off, every few hours, daily or weekly at a set time; production servers back up daily at 02:00 unless changed.
  • Restore needs your password, first saves the current data as a restore point, and signs everyone out.

Server restart (system administrator only)

Back up, update and restart saves a backup, gets the newest code, checks it and restarts. If the update fails, nothing restarts. Production servers can't erase their data.

8. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Code or signing email didn't arriveCheck junk/spam and send a new one. Senders can use Email from Outlook. Still missing: raise a sign-in help ticket.
“Account locked”Wait 15 minutes, or ask an administrator to unlock it.
“Signing is locked”Wait 30 minutes, then try again or send yourself a new temporary password.
“You are logged in as … but this document was sent to …”Log out and sign in with the email the agreement was sent to.
“This link is no longer valid”You've already signed. The signed PDF is emailed when everyone has signed; with an account, use Log in to view.
Upload refusedUse PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text or common image files. iPhone HEIC photos: share as JPG.
Everyone signed but no signed PDFSignatures are kept. An administrator opens the agreement and clicks Retry completion.
