Policies

Method365 is the electronic signature system operated by the company. These policies explain how electronic signing works, what information is collected and how it is protected.

1. Electronic Record and Signature Disclosure

Before you sign, you are asked to agree to use electronic records and signatures. Please read this disclosure first. By ticking the consent box and signing, you confirm that you have read it and agree to it.

Your agreement to do business electronically

You agree that documents, notices and disclosures may be provided to you electronically, and that your electronic signature has the same legal effect as your handwritten signature. Your consent covers the document you are signing and the related notices for it.

Paper copies

You may ask for a paper copy of any document you signed electronically, free of charge, by contacting the sender or the address in section 6. You receive the completed, signed PDF by email once all parties have signed; you can print or save it.

Withdrawing your consent

You may withdraw consent before you sign by choosing Decline on the signing page, or by telling the sender. Withdrawing consent does not affect documents you have already signed. After withdrawing, the sender may ask you to sign on paper instead.

What you need

  • A current web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) with JavaScript turned on
  • An email address you can access, to receive signing links, temporary passwords and completed documents
  • A PDF reader (built into most browsers) to view, save or print documents

Keeping your email address up to date

Documents are sent to the email address the sender entered. If it changes, tell the sender so that future documents reach you. Account holders can ask their administrator to update it.

2. Identity verification and security

  • Only the invited person can sign. A document can be signed only with the email address it was sent to. Having the link is not enough.
  • Account holders sign in with their email, password and a one-time code emailed to them, and enter their password again when they sign.
  • Recipients without an account receive a temporary password by email. It works once, for that document only, and expires after 60 minutes. The sender never sees it.
  • After five wrong passwords, signing is locked for 30 minutes and the sender is told.
  • Never share your password, sign-in codes, temporary passwords or signing links. Staff of the company will never ask you for them.
  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so no one, including administrators, can read them.
  • Completed documents are digitally signed and locked. Any change afterwards is detected, and anyone can check a document on the Verify page.

If you think someone has used your email or signing link without permission, contact us immediately (section 6).

3. Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Identity details: your name and email address, and, if you enter them, your title and company.
  • Your signature: the signature and initials you draw, type or upload, and the reason for signing you choose.
  • Document content: the documents sent to you, the values you fill in and any files you attach.
  • Evidence of signing: date and time, IP address, browser and device type, how your identity was checked, and each step you take (for example when you opened, signed or declined a document).
  • Email delivery: invitation emails contain a small image that records when the email was first opened.
  • Account holders: sign-in times, failed sign-in attempts and the actions you take in the system.

Why we use it

To deliver documents for signature, confirm who signed, produce the signed document and its audit trail, keep the system secure, and meet legal and regulatory record-keeping duties. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Saved details

To save you time, your title, company and adopted signature may be offered again the next time a document is sent to the same email address. You always confirm them before they are used.

Who can see it

  • The sender, the other signers of the same document, and authorised staff of the company.
  • Service providers that run the system for us, such as the hosting provider and the email provider, only as needed to deliver the service.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Cookies

Only one cookie is used: it keeps account holders signed in. It is not used for tracking or advertising. People who sign without an account need no cookie.

Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you and ask for corrections. Because signed documents and audit trails are legal records, they cannot be changed or deleted while they must be kept (section 4). Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under data protection law; contact us to use them.

4. Records, audit trail and retention

  • Every agreement has a permanent ID and an audit trail of every action, protected so that any later change or deletion is detected.
  • Agreements that have been sent cannot be deleted, only cancelled, so the record stays complete.
  • Signed documents, audit trails and sign-in records are kept for as long as the company's record-keeping policy and the law require. Draft documents that were never sent may be discarded.
  • Data is backed up regularly. Backups are protected like the live system.

5. Terms of use

  • Use Method365 only for genuine business documents and only for documents you are entitled to send or sign.
  • Sign only documents sent to you, and only as yourself. Signing for someone else is not allowed.
  • Account holders are responsible for keeping their password and sign-in codes private, and must report any suspected misuse.
  • Do not try to get around security checks, access other people's documents, or disrupt the service.
  • Some documents (for example wills or certain property and court documents) may need a handwritten or specially certified signature. Ask the sender if you are unsure.
  • Access may be suspended where these terms are broken or to protect the service.

6. Contact and changes

Questions, paper copies, corrections or security concerns: contact the sender of your document.

If these policies change, the version and effective date above are updated. Each signature records the version of the disclosure that the signer agreed to.
